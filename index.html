Our Story

Two spice routes, one ambitious weekend.

Centuries ago, ships carrying chilli, galangal and turmeric reached a tiny port that became Singapore. Half a world away, corn, cacao and chilli were building an empire of their own in Mexico. Two spice routes that never officially met, until now.

This National Day, hawker-stall heritage meets taco-truck swagger in the same pot. One shared rempah base does double duty across both cuisines. It's a small tribute to a nation built by people who took what they were given and made it their own.

Come taste it, one birria-rendang quesataco at a time.

Behind The Scenes

The Journey of the Feast

Three days, one very full fridge, one moment of quiet panic. Here's the saga.

🌙
Friday Night

The Marinade Begins

The beef and lamb are seasoned and sealed. Lamb gets a rub of birria spice, beef stays clean for its rendang destiny. The feast begins quietly.

🔥
Saturday

The Big Blend

One shared spice base splits three ways into achar, sambal and the birria foundation. Both proteins go into a slow 24-hour bath. Both rendang sauces come together. Two consommés are born.

🎉
Sunday

The Big Reveal

The proteins come out of their long bath, rest, then get shredded into the lamb consommé with every drop of bag juice. Everything is packed cold and driven to the party.
