Our Story

Two spice routes, one ambitious weekend.

Trade ships once carried chilli, galangal and turmeric into the tiny port that became Singapore, while half a world away those same peppers were reshaping kitchens across Mexico. Two spice routes running in parallel for centuries, never quite crossing, until this table brings them together.

This National Day, hawker-stall heritage sits down with taco-truck swagger, sharing one rempah base that does double duty across both cuisines. Pull up a chair and taste it for yourself, one birria-rendang quesataco at a time.
